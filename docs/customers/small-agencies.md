# Segment 2 — Small agencies (2–15 people)

**Priority: SECOND. Opens after segment 1 produces repeat buyers.**

## Who

Boutique marketing, design, dev, PR and recruiting shops. A founder-operator who still does
client work, plus a handful of employees or contractors.

## Economics

- Same hourly logic as solo consultants, **multiplied by headcount**.
- The founder decides, and decides fast. No procurement.
- This is where **per-seat pricing** first makes sense — the natural bridge from one-time
  purchase to recurring revenue.

## Their actual pain

Different from segment 1. It is not "I lack time," it is **inconsistency**:

- Every team member uses AI differently, at different quality levels.
- Junior output needs heavy senior review.
- Knowledge lives in one person's head and leaves when they do.
- Client deliverables vary in quality depending on who touched them.

## What they will pay for

**A shared standard.** "Everyone on the team does discovery/reporting/QA the same way, at the
level of our best person." That is worth far more than time savings, and it is a story only a
*maintained* library can tell.

## What they will NOT pay for

- Anything that needs a rollout plan or training day.
- Anything a junior can't use unsupervised on day one.

## Why this segment matters strategically

It is the first place the product stops being a file and starts being **infrastructure** —
which is the honest justification for a subscription. If agencies renew, the SaaS in Phase 3
has a reason to exist. If they don't, we stay a one-time-purchase business, and that's fine.

## How to reach them

- Referral from segment 1 — solo consultants know agency founders.
- Agency-owner communities and podcasts.
- LinkedIn, targeting founder/partner titles at 2–15 person shops.

## Open questions

- [ ] Do they want seats, or one license the founder distributes internally?
- [ ] Is "consistency" or "speed" the stronger buying trigger? (Test both in copy.)
- [ ] What price per seat stops feeling casual and starts needing a decision? (Probably ~$15/mo.)
