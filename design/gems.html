Emerald & ruby

The stash metaphor, with the second colour given a job rather than left as decoration: emerald is the accent, and ruby marks the ones you built — the rare stones.

Ruby can't be text. A true ruby red is #C4304A at 3.65:1 on this ground — under the 4.5:1 minimum. #D93C57 is 4.46:1 and still fails. Only pinker reds pass, and they stop looking like ruby. So ruby is a fill (white on it is 5.4:1), and #E85C75 is held back for the rare case where it has to be type.

Second thing: red and green are the pair colour-blind readers lose first. That's fine here only because they never encode the same axis — emerald is chrome, ruby means mine, and the mine pill says the word too. Don't ever let ruby alone carry a meaning.

Names that fit the metaphor

Your own word was “stash”, and it's the best of these.

pick

stash.tools — your word. One syllable, developer-native, and git stash is a friendly collision rather than a confusing one: things you set aside on purpose. Works with or without the gem reading.

Stronger on “permanent place” than stash is, since a vault implies kept rather than parked. Heavier, and slightly crypto-adjacent in this field.
