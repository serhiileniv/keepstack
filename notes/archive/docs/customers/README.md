# Who we sell to

Four segments are in scope. **They are not worked in parallel.** One operator cannot write
four sets of messaging, so we sequence them and only open the next when the current one has
produced paying strangers.

| Order | Segment | Willingness to pay | Reachability | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Solo consultants](solo-consultants.md) → **[niche: fractional CTO](niche-fractional-cto.md)** | **High** | High | Bill $100–200/hr, buy time back, expense it without approval. Fastest path to Rung 1. |
| 2 | [Small agencies (2–15)](small-agencies.md) | **High** | Medium | Same economics as #1 but multiplied by headcount. Natural upsell into seats. |
| 3 | [SMB marketing managers](smb-marketing-managers.md) | High | Medium | Real budget line, low technical skill, want done-for-you. Needs different, non-technical packaging. |
| 4 | [Small dev teams](small-dev-teams.md) | Medium | **High** | Easiest to reach, loudest, cheapest. Great for distribution, weak for revenue. |

## Why dev teams are last despite being easiest to reach

They can build it themselves, they expect open source, and they will argue with the price in
public. They are still worth targeting — but as a **distribution channel**, not a revenue line.
Free artifacts aimed at dev teams generate the reach that sells to segments 1–3.

Do not confuse "they engaged with my post" with "they will pay."

## The segment we explicitly reject

**AI hobbyists and prompt collectors.** High engagement, zero conversion. They will fill a
Discord, star a repo, and never spend a dollar. Every hour spent on them is an hour not spent
on segment 1. If our audience starts tilting hobbyist, that is a warning sign, not traction.

## Niche locked

Segment 1 is narrowed to **technical consultants & fractional CTOs**

## The one qualifying question

Before writing anything for a segment, answer this for that segment:

> **What is the specific, recurring, named task that costs them more than $49 of their own
> time each month?**

If we cannot answer that in one concrete sentence, we do not understand the segment well
enough to sell to it, and no amount of good content will fix that.

For segment 1 this is already answered in the [niche brief](niche-fractional-cto.md).
