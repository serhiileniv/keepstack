# Segment 1 — Solo consultants & independent professionals

**Priority: FIRST. This is the segment that gets us to Rung 2 (10 users / $100).**

> **Narrowed to a specific niche:** technical consultants & fractional CTOs. See
> [ADR-008](../decisions/ADR-008-niche.md) and the working brief in
> [niche-fractional-cto.md](niche-fractional-cto.md). This file stays as the general
> segment-level reasoning; the brief is what Phase 1 is written against.

## Who

Independent consultants, freelance strategists, fractional execs, solo lawyers/accountants,
independent recruiters. One person, no team, no IT department, no procurement.

## Economics

- Bills **$100–200/hr**, sometimes far more.
- A $49 product that saves 1 hour/month pays for itself in the first month, forever.
- **No approval needed.** They are the buyer, the user and the budget holder. This removes
  the single biggest cause of stalled small sales.
- Expenses it against revenue without thinking about it.

## Their actual pain

Not "I want to use AI better." The real pain is:

- Proposals, scoping docs and SOWs written from scratch every time.
- Client research and briefing before every first call.
- Turning a 60-minute call into notes, actions and a follow-up email.
- Reporting and recaps that clients expect but won't pay for.

All of it is **unbillable admin around billable work**. That is the wedge.

## What they will pay for

A workflow that takes a named unbillable task from 45 minutes to 5. Concrete, single-task,
demonstrable. They do not want a system; they want the specific thing done.

## What they will NOT pay for

- Anything requiring setup beyond copy-paste.
- Anything framed as "learn prompt engineering." They don't want to learn; they want output.
- A subscription, at first. Sell one-time, earn the subscription later.

## How to reach them

- LinkedIn — this is where they actually live, unlike segment 4.
- Niche communities for their discipline (not AI communities).
- Their own complaints, in public, about admin work. Search for those and reply usefully.

## Message that works

> "The three hours a week you spend on proposals, done in twenty minutes. Verified, updated
> when the models change."

## Message that fails

> "500 AI prompts for consultants."

## Open questions to answer in customer conversations

- [ ] Which unbillable task do they hate most? (Ask; don't assume it's proposals.)
- [ ] Do they already use Claude/ChatGPT daily, or is onboarding part of the job?
- [ ] What have they already paid for in this space, and did they use it after week one?
