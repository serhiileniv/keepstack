# Segment 3 — SMB marketing managers

**Priority: THIRD. Requires a different product surface — do not open early.**

## Who

The one marketing person (or team of two or three) at a 20–200 person company. Not technical.
Owns a budget line. Under constant pressure to produce more output than the headcount supports.

## Economics

- **Has real budget** and is used to buying tools — this is the segment most accustomed to
  paying for software.
- Spends company money, not their own, which raises the ceiling and lowers the friction.
- May need a manager's sign-off above ~$100. Below that, they just buy it.

## Their actual pain

- Volume. Content, campaigns, briefs, reports — more asked of them than is possible.
- Justifying spend and results upward, monthly.
- They are being *told* to "use AI" without any guidance on how.

## What they will pay for

**Done-for-you, not build-your-own.** They want the finished workflow, in the tool they
already use, producing output they can put in front of their boss on Friday. The word
"configure" loses this sale.

## What makes this segment expensive to serve

They need the **highest-touch packaging** of any segment:

- Zero technical setup. Nothing involving a terminal, a repo, or a config file.
- Screenshots and video, not READMEs.
- A different vocabulary — "campaign brief workflow," never "agent skill."

That is why they are third. Serving them well means building a second front-end for the same
content, and that only makes sense once segments 1 and 2 have funded it.

## How to reach them

- LinkedIn, marketing communities, newsletters they already read.
- Their peers, not AI circles.

## Risk specific to this segment

They churn when they change jobs, and they change jobs often. Anything recurring sold here
should be priced expecting shorter retention than segments 1 and 2.

## Open questions

- [ ] Which tool do they actually live in? (Answer determines whether we even can serve them.)
- [ ] Is the buyer the marketing manager or their director?
- [ ] Does a non-technical wrapper cost more to build than this segment returns? Answer honestly
      before opening it.
