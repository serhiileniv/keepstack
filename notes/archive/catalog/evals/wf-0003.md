# Eval — Risk and tech-debt inventory

## How to re-run

1. Fresh session, fixture repo at the pinned SHA.
2. Execute steps 2, 4, 5 and 6. Steps 1 and 3 need a human engagement and are skipped; the
   inbox is replaced by the committed observation list in `examples/wf-0003.md`.
3. Compare the resulting ranking to the committed reference ranking.

## Inputs

`FIXTURE_REPO` @ `FIXTURE_SHA`, plus the committed raw observation list.

## Pass criteria

- [ ] The mechanical sweeps (step 2) find every planted item in the reference list: the
      dependency advisory, the credential in git history, the unauthenticated entry point, the
      unretried external call, the missing constraint.
- [ ] Every inventory item carries consequence, trigger **and** cost. Any item missing one is a
      fail.
- [ ] No item ranked in the top five has "nothing measurable" as its consequence.
- [ ] The trigger rule holds: nothing with an *unusual event* trigger outranks anything with an
      *already happening* or *routine event* trigger.
- [ ] Top five overlaps the reference top five by ≥ 4 of 5. Ordering may differ; membership
      should not.
- [ ] The credential-in-history item is flagged as out-of-band, not placed in the ranked list.

- [ ] frontier model
- [ ] cheaper model
