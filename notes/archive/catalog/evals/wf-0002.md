# Eval — The week-one written assessment

## How to re-run

1. Fresh session. Inputs are the **committed** `map.md` and inventory from the fixture run —
   not a fresh orientation. This eval tests the writing pass in isolation.
2. Execute `wf-0002` steps 1–5. Step 6 is a meeting and is out of scope.
3. Time it; record in the run log.

## Inputs

`examples/wf-0001.md` (map + unknowns) and `examples/wf-0003.md` (ranked inventory), both at
their committed versions.

## Pass criteria

- [ ] Output is 2–4 pages. Longer is a fail, not a note.
- [ ] All six sections present, in order.
- [ ] Section 4 has **exactly five** items, each with a business consequence sentence and a
      rough cost. An item without a consequence is a fail.
- [ ] Section 2 names at least two specific things that work, with file or component names.
- [ ] The verification pass (step 4) flags every number and every absence claim. Checked against
      the pinned list of planted claims in `examples/wf-0002.md` — **at least one of which is
      deliberately false.** Missing the planted false claim is a fail.
- [ ] Zero instances of: "best practice", "leverage", "modernise", "robust", "scalable".
- [ ] Section 5 sequences the first two weeks concretely.

## Models to test against

- [ ] frontier model
- [ ] cheaper model
