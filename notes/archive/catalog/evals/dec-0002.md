# Eval — How deep to go in week one

Expiry trigger: *the cost of whole-repo depth falls far enough that breadth and depth stop
trading off.* If a full deep pass becomes cheap, the call moves from B toward C and this artifact
is wrong.

## How to re-run

1. Fresh session, fixture repo at the pinned SHA.
2. Attempt a **full-depth pass** with a current frontier model: every entry point traced to the
   datastore, not just the money path.
3. Record wall-clock time, token cost, and — the part that matters — **accuracy**, by spot-
   checking 10 claims from the deep pass against the code.

## Inputs

`FIXTURE_REPO` @ `FIXTURE_SHA`. The same 10 spot-check claims each run, listed in
`examples/dec-0002.md`.

## Pass criteria

The decision **still holds** (pass) if any of these is true:

- [ ] The full-depth pass takes more than one working day of operator attention, **or**
- [ ] Spot-check accuracy is below 9/10, **or**
- [ ] The pass cannot be completed without manual chunking or repeated re-prompting.

The decision is **stale** (fail) if a full-depth pass over the fixture completes in a few hours
of mostly unattended agent time at 9/10 or better accuracy. That is the world in which the
three-day time-box is leaving value on the table, and this artifact needs rewriting toward
option C.

## Models to test against

- [ ] every new frontier model on release — this is one of the two most model-sensitive
      artifacts in the catalogue
- [ ] cheaper model, for the cost comparison
