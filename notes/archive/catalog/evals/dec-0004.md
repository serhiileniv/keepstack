# Eval — Which tech debt to raise, and which to absorb

No model dependency. This artifact expires on **engagement shape**, and its eval is a review
against real engagements rather than a session run.

## How to re-run

Quarterly, not monthly. Review every assessment delivered since the last run.

1. For each: how many items were raised? Was the cap held?
2. What did the client actually fund, and was it on the raised list?
3. Did any client ask *"is this everything?"* or push back on the length?
4. Did anything you put in the appendix later cause a problem you were held responsible for?
5. Did any engagement type change — due diligence, interim, regulated?

## Inputs

The delivered assessments and the follow-up emails from step 6 of `wf-0002`. **This eval is
impossible without those emails**, which is a second reason to send them.

## Pass criteria

- [ ] The cap of five held without feeling artificial in ≥ 3 of the last 4 engagements.
- [ ] In ≥ 3 of 4, the client funded something from the raised list. If they consistently funded
      something *else*, the ranking model in `wf-0003` is wrong, not this artifact.
- [ ] No appendix item became a "you never told us" conversation.
- [ ] Fewer than 2 clients asked whether the list was complete. Two or more means five is the
      wrong number for the clients you now have.
